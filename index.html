About PAYE Tax

PAYE (Pay As You Earn) is a system for collecting income tax from employees' salaries or wages. Employers deduct tax from employees' pay and submit it to ZIMRA monthly. This section tracks all clients with PAYE obligations.

About QPDS Tax

QPDS (Quarterly Provisional Declaration System) requires businesses to declare and pay estimated income tax every quarter. Deadlines are: March 25th, June 25th, September 25th, and December 20th. This section tracks quarterly submissions for all clients.

About VAT Tax

VAT (Value Added Tax) is a consumption tax charged on goods and services. Registered businesses must submit VAT returns and payments monthly by the 25th of the following month. This section tracks VAT compliance for all registered clients.
